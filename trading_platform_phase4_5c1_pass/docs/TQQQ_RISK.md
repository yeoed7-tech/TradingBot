# TQQQ risk and research controls

[ProShares TQQQ](https://www.proshares.com/our-etfs/leveraged-and-inverse/tqqq) targets 3× the Nasdaq-100 daily result before fund expenses. Multi-day outcome is path dependent: a +10% index day followed by −9.09% leaves the index approximately flat but a hypothetical +30%, −27.27% product loses about 5.45%. No eventual recovery is assumed. The history begins at fund inception; it does not include a synthetic dot-com or 2008 drawdown. Fund expenses are embedded in traded prices; do not charge the expense ratio a second time.

The major risks are overnight gaps, leveraged drawdowns, volatility compounding, illiquid closing auctions, insufficient cash after repeated buying, and long periods below the equity peak. Holding USD also changes translated KRW results. Cash distributions are modeled as receivables because payment dates were unavailable. Taxes, withholding, actual FX spreads and broker-specific fees must be verified before any further gate.

Research caps: allocated cycle capital, tranche count, daily intent count, USD reserve, max buy amount and common gross/symbol exposure. Default loss cutoffs are intentionally nonbinding in historical simulation to expose the actual drawdown; malformed session data and an explicit common kill still stop new exposure. Share count and total cost are separate across forward splits. No borrowing or automatic replenishment. Existing positions can exceed a mark-to-market cap through appreciation; the cap restricts added exposure, not forced sales.

Runtime protections to prove before live: stale and out-of-order quotes, clock/holiday/early-close mismatch, uncertain submission, unrecognized holdings, wrong cash bucket, partial/duplicate or unexpectedly large fills, quote outage and reconciliation mismatch. Stale WS and reconciliation checks are implemented for read observation; production kill and confirmed external fills are not validated. Daily OHLC does not establish actual LOC fill probability, cutoff or execution capacity.
